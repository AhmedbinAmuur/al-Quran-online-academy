Student Rules & Regulations

A respectful and disciplined learning environment

The registration form states the following expectations for students and parents/guardians.

01

Attendance & Punctuality

Attend scheduled classes regularly and join online classes early. Inform the teacher in advance when unable to attend.

02

Islamic Etiquette

Respect Allah's Book, fellow students, and teachers. Kindness and honesty are expected; offensive or inappropriate language is prohibited.

03

Dress & Materials

Dress modestly. Keep the Qur'an, notebook, exercise books, pens, and required textbooks ready. Complete homework on time.

04

Privacy

Do not record, photograph, or share lessons without permission. Class links, passwords, and meeting IDs must remain private.

05

Parent/Guardian Role

For children, parents/guardians should provide a quiet study place, help younger children join on time, and encourage revision and homework.
