Registration

Tell us about the student

The registration form helps the academy place each student in the appropriate class.

Student Information

The form asks for the student's full name, age, gender, parent/guardian name, primary mobile number, optional second number, optional email address, and city/country of residence.

Learning Information

You will provide whether you are working, who will attend the classes, your preferred study time, how much Qur'an you have memorized, and how many pages you can read or memorize daily.

Choose Your Course

Select from Hifdh, Tajweed, Qur'an Reading, Writing, Hadith, Du'as, Tauheed, Seerah, Fiqh, or All of the Above.

Agreement

Before submitting, the form asks you to agree to the registration and tuition fees and includes a parent/guardian declaration for the information provided.
